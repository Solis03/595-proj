We test how redundancy, confidence language, and Markdown emphasis affect pairwise judgments by three small Qwen3 models. We measure verdict stability on 323 CALM questions and correctness on 462 labeled JudgeBench pairs, applying the same edits to correct and incorrect candidates in separate conditions. With a brief prompt, duplication changes 22.6\% of Qwen3-4B verdicts on CALM. On JudgeBench, duplicating the incorrect answer switches 94 of 499 initially correct verdicts to the wrong answer. Net accuracy changes from 53.65\% to 53.14\%. The paired interval includes zero. We also compare a content rubric, order pooling, and weight precision.
